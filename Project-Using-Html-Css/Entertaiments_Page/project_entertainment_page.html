Popular Streaming and Online Platforms

YouTube

YouTube is a video sharing platform where users can watch, upload, and share videos. It is used for education, entertainment, music, and tutorials.

Amazon

Amazon is an online shopping platform where people can buy products like electronics, clothes, books, and more. It also provides streaming through Amazon Prime Video.

Netflix

Netflix is a popular streaming service that offers movies, web series, TV shows, and original content. It is used worldwide for entertainment.

Instagram

Instagram is a social media platform used to share photos, videos, reels, and stories. People use it for communication and entertainment.

Disney+ Hotstar

Disney+ Hotstar provides movies, TV shows, sports, and Disney content. It is popular for cricket streaming and Indian shows.

Spotify

Spotify is a music streaming platform where users can listen to songs, albums, and podcasts online.

Sony LIV

Sony LIV offers Indian TV shows, movies, live sports, and web series.

ZEE5

ZEE5 is an Indian streaming platform that provides movies, shows, and original web series in multiple languages.

MX Player

MX Player is a video player and streaming service that offers movies, web series, and TV shows.

JioCinema

JioCinema provides movies, web series, and live sports for Jio users.

Apple TV+

Apple TV+ is a streaming service by Apple that provides original movies and web series.

Hulu

Hulu is a streaming platform that offers TV shows, movies, and original content.

HBO Max

HBO Max provides movies, TV shows, and popular series like Game of Thrones.

Crunchyroll

Crunchyroll is a streaming platform mainly used for watching anime and Japanese shows.
